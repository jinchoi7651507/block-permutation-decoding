- **Rules (fixed now; also the script's docstring):** rows kept at their first occurrence per (eid, region, label, row type); pilot rows ignored; completed prefix = the largest m with sessions 0..m−1 finished, skipped or errored (errors are deterministic, no freedom). Rejection = p < 0.05 strictly; None/NaN p excluded per cell and counted. Aggregation: per region-session (share over its usable draws, any number ≥ 1; those with < 20 usable draws counted), then the mean per mouse, then over mice; paired contrasts the same way on per-draw differences with both values usable (per region-session first, then per mouse). Interval: mouse-cluster BCa, B = 20,000 (any other B refused unless `--synthetic`), seed 20261023, one generator for every interval in output order, acceleration from the mouse jackknife, z0 from the mid-rank of the observed value among the bootstrap values (ties within 1e-12; scipy's convention); fewer than 2 mice → no interval. 95% interval for every quantity; Bonferroni 98.3% for the three primary contrasts F, V, S only. Decisions: a contrast is "positive" if its 95% lower bound > 0 (F, S, V); a rate is "inflated" if its 95% lower bound > 10%; V gets no decision when the realism response makes Q2 descriptive. Secondary contrasts (§7; same pairing and interval; no decision): P1, P4 and PS-fixed minus PS-refit (S-main, fake labels); S-block = P2-block minus P2 (fake labels); P1 minus IID-P1. V: 2 × 2 verdict table, discordant counts with an exact two-sided binomial p (descriptive: region-sessions are not independent), and the §8 cross-tab: P2-only vs all other real-label region-sessions with both verdicts, by their own fake-label P2 share > 10%, ≤ 10% or no fake value. Q2 rates mouse-weighted with intervals and region-session-weighted; the share of p in 0.03–0.07 for Q1 and Q2 rows; denominators per cell (mice, region-sessions, usable p, NaN/None p); the PS-refit row on fake labels labelled as the sanity check; positive-control table with mice per group; Extended Data region counts over usable p only.
