## 2. Blocks and preprocessing (`code/preprocess.py`; DESIGN §3)
- **Blocks:** condition markers (S200 eyes open, S210 eyes closed) repeat every 2 s inside a condition. A block is a run of identical markers; it runs from its first marker to the earlier of the next block's first marker and its own last marker + 2 s. Eligibility required 16 alternating blocks of 50–80 s.
- **Crop and resample:** from the first block onset to the last block end, then 250 Hz.
- **Cleaned (primary):** band-pass 1–45 Hz (MNE FIR defaults) and 50 Hz notch. The gaps between blocks (up to about 20 s) are annotated BAD_gap and left out of the channel statistics and the ICA fit. Bad channels = peak-to-peak below 1 µV inside the blocks (flat), or a **local** robust z of log variance above 5: each channel's log variance minus the median log variance of its 4 nearest non-flat neighbours (standard_1005 positions), turned into a robust z over the non-flat channels (median, 1.4826 × MAD), one-sided. **Reading of DESIGN's "z-scored log variance > 5":** the plain z cannot flag anything when one channel is flat (log variance −∞) and can never exceed sqrt((61 − k)/k) with k equally bad channels (4.4 for k = 3); a global robust z flags spatially broad physiological signals (posterior alpha in eyes-closed blocks, frontal blinks), which are the positive control and must stay. In a stress test with FCz-referenced synthetic fields (as LEMON is recorded; EEG re-review; rerun with the reviewer's script) the local rule found a single channel at 9× variance always and at 3× variance in 25–42% of cases, with 0.5–1.5 false flags per subject, all at Fp1/Fp2 (interpolating a good blink channel is label-free; ICA still finds the blink component on AF/F channels); a temporary average reference or a window-median variance moved the false flags to O1/Oz/O2 and lost power, so the rule is kept. The number of subjects with Fp1/Fp2 or O1/Oz/O2 flagged is reported. (An earlier sentence here, "90% of 3× variance channels with 0.15 false flags per subject", came from a test without the FCz reference and with 3× amplitude, i.e. 9× variance.) Flagged channels are interpolated before the average reference of the 61 EEG channels. ICA with 20 components (FastICA, random_state 20261021) fitted on the 1–45 Hz data (DESIGN: "fit on the 1 Hz high-passed data"), components with |r| > 0.5 against VEOG removed; epochs with EEG peak-to-peak above 150 µV rejected after ICA.
- **Minimal (sensitivity):** band-pass 1–45 Hz, average reference, 300 µV, no notch, interpolation or ICA.
- **Epochs:** non-overlapping 2-s windows inside each block, skipping the first 5 s and the last 2 s (sensitivity 2 s and 2 s): 26 windows per 60-s block before rejection.
- **Features:** log band power per EEG channel (1–4, 4–8, 8–13, 13–30, 30–45 Hz; Welch, 1-s windows, 50% overlap) → 305.
- **QC per subject:** blocks and durations, first condition, bad and flat channels with their local robust z (and whether the MAD was zero), ICA iterations and whether ICA converged (iterations below the maximum), VEOG amplitude, pseudo-HEOG (F7 − F8) correlations of the components (logged only; LEMON has no HEOG), the 50 Hz line ratio of the cleaned spectrum (DESIGN's "PSD checked"), ICA correlations and removed components, epochs kept and rejected per block and variant, posterior log alpha EC − EO (O1, Oz, O2, PO3, POz, PO4, PO7, PO8, P3, Pz, P4) and whether EC > EO (DESIGN §2, E12: a subject failing it stays in the fake-label tables and is left out of the positive-control plot only).

## 3. Analysis set (`code/run_2b.py`)
- **Failures and replacements (DESIGN §2–§3):** a subject whose preprocessing fails (the code stops, or the recording is unusable: not 16 blocks, or a channel set different from the 61 + VEOG) is replaced by the next subject of the same stratum in the stamped seeded order, **skipping the 40 drawn and the 5 pilot subjects** (`eligibility/REPLACEMENT-ORDER.txt`, hashed; young starts at sub-010216, old at sub-010092); the replacement takes the failed subject's index in every seed, is downloaded and preprocessed with the same code, and is reported. A subject with fewer than 20 epochs in any block (DESIGN §3) is dropped without replacement and listed.
- The analysis set is fixed on the primary variant (cleaned, 5/2 s): a subject dropped there is dropped in every variant and readout; the other variants use their own smallest per-block count (reported).
- **Equal epochs per block (reading of DESIGN §6 S9):** every block of a subject is subsampled (seeded, `default_rng([2026, 1, subject index])`) to that subject's smallest per-block epoch count (kept count stored per row). Both classes then have equal epoch counts in every assignment and every null draw, and chance accuracy is 0.5. The statistic remains the mean of fold accuracies (shuffled folds are not class-balanced fold by fold); this replaces computing balanced accuracy inside the rules, which would change the stamped step 2 rule code. It discards epochs (down to as few as 20 of 26 per block).
- Subject index = 0-based position in `eligibility/SUBJECTS.txt`.
- **EC/EO codes:** every marker file starts with S210 (EC-first 40/40 in the main set). If fewer than 25% of the subjects pass the alpha check (posterior alpha EC > EO), the two codes are taken as swapped for all subjects and this is reported; if 25–75% pass, the codes are taken as unreliable per subject, the state-only arms are reported as "odd blocks" and "even blocks" rather than eyes closed/open, and this is reported; otherwise single failing subjects are handled as DESIGN §2 (E12).

## 4. Arms (DESIGN §4)
| Arm | Assignments tested per subject | P5 null set |
|---|---|---|
| balanced | 50 of the 4,900 (class 1 = 4 EC + 4 EO blocks); draw d uses `default_rng([2026, 2, s, d])`, redrawn until neither the assignment nor its A/B swap was drawn before | the 4,900 |
| ec_only, eo_only | all 35 assignments distinct up to the swap (4 + 4 of the state's 8 blocks; only that state's epochs) | the 70 |
| fixed_half, fixed_aabb, fixed_abba | one each: blocks 1–8 vs 9–16; block pairs A A B B A A B B; block pairs A B B A A B B A (each class 4 EC + 4 EO by construction) | the 4,900 |
| long120 | the 35 distinct assignments of 8 blocks of 120 s (consecutive pairs) | the 70 |
| pos_full | EC vs EO (pipeline check, expected at ceiling) | all 12,870 8 + 8 assignments |
| pos_graded | EC vs EO, 50 seeded weakenings (`default_rng([2026, 5, s, d])`) with the rule fixed by the pilot (§5); one if the rule has no random part | all 12,870 |
| balanced_graded | the balanced arm's 50 fake assignments on data weakened by the same rule (`default_rng([2026, 7, s, d])`), S-main; the null for size-adjusted power of pos_graded | the 4,900 |
- DESIGN §4 gives "50 assignments per subject" for the balanced arm and the assignment counts (70, 35 distinct) for the state-only and long-block arms; those arms use all 35 distinct assignments rather than repeating swapped ones.
- **P3 runs:** runs of 4 consecutive blocks of the arm (DESIGN §5): 4 runs with 16 blocks, 2 runs in the 8-block arms. A run without both classes gives the CoSMoMVPA error (reported, both denominators). In fixed_half and fixed_aabb every run holds one class, so P3 is undefined by construction (printed as such).
- **P5 in the enumerated arms (ec_only, eo_only, long120):** testing every member of the reference set makes P5's per-subject rate fixed by construction (about 3.6%, at most about 3/35); it is noted, not read as validity, and P2 − P5 there is not interpreted. The smallest numerical Monte Carlo p is 1/201 (DESIGN §4's "about 1/35" refers to the exact enumeration).
- **S-block folds:** GroupKFold(4) with 16 equal blocks puts blocks {b, b + 4, b + 8, b + 12} together; with alternating EC/EO every S-block test fold holds one state.
- **Splits:** S-main (shuffled KFold 5), S-tool (unshuffled StratifiedKFold 5, re-split per permuted label vector), S-block (GroupKFold(4) by block of the arm, deterministic).
- **Rules and readout:** step 2 `sim.py` rules P1–P5 and `sim.pipeline()` (k = 20), 200 permutation draws. Sensitivity (balanced arm, S-main only): k = all, LDA without selection, the cleaned 2/2-s variant and the minimal pipeline.
- Seeds: folds and P2 `default_rng([2026, 3, s, a, arm index])` (the same integer seeds the KFold shuffle and P2's permutations); rules `default_rng([2026, 4, s, a, arm index, split index])`.
- Stored per row: every p, P3 error flag, observed accuracy, the 200 P5 null accuracies, correlation of the labels with block index, the integer seed, subject/arm/split indices, kept epochs per block. **Deviation from DESIGN §5 "per-draw accuracies stored":** only P5's null accuracies are stored; P2–P4 nulls are internal to the stamped step 2 code and are reproducible from the stored seeds.
- Sensitivity readouts (balanced arm, S-main): k = all, LDA without selection, the main pipeline without the 30–45 Hz features (EMG; EEG review), the cleaned 2/2-s variant, the minimal pipeline.
- **Cut order if compute runs short (fixed now):** sensitivity readouts first, then the minimal pipeline, then the S-tool column of the state-only and long-block arms; the balanced arm, the fixed-order arms and the positive controls are never cut.

## 5. Graded positive control: pilot (`code/graded_pilot.py`; DESIGN §4 "rule chosen on 5 pilot subjects")
- **Pilot subjects:** the next 3 young and 2 old subjects in the stamped seeded order after the 40 drawn (`eligibility/PILOT-SUBJECTS.txt`, hashed; proportions as the main draw). Not in any main table. Downloaded with `code/download_pilot.sh`.
- **Weakening ladder (fixed now), in order:** R1 frontal channels (Fp1, Fp2, AF7, AF3, AFz, AF4, AF8, F7, F5, F3, F1, Fz, F2, F4, F6, F8; all five bands), all epochs; R2 all channels, 4 random epochs per block; R4 frontal, 4 per block; R3 all channels, 2 per block; R5 frontal, 2 per block; then a mixing ladder (EEG review: EC vs EO may stay at ceiling under R1–R5): Q30, Q35, Q40, Q45, Q48 = a fraction q of each block's epochs exchanged with random epochs of the paired opposite-state block (blocks 2j and 2j + 1; exchange, not copy, so no epoch appears twice; at most (n − 1)/2 epochs of an n-epoch block, so no step reaches half a block), all channels and epochs, labels unchanged. DESIGN §4 gives frontal channels and epoch subsampling as examples of weakening; the mixing steps are added here.
- **Pilot:** per rule and pilot subject, 20 seeded weakenings (`default_rng([2026, 6, s, d])`; one for R1, which has no random part), EC vs EO, S-main, P5 only. Power pooled and per subject.
- **Choice:** the first rule in the ladder with pooled power in [0.20, 0.90] and per-subject power strictly between 0.05 and 0.95 in at least 3 of the 5 pilot subjects; R1 has one test per pilot subject (no random part), so its per-subject power is 0 or 1 and it cannot meet the per-subject condition; it is kept in the ladder for reporting only. If none, the graded control is reported as not achieved and pos_graded / balanced_graded are not run. Written to `runs/graded_pilot.json` and committed before the main run. The main-run power is targeted by the pilot, not guaranteed (DESIGN §7 "by construction" is read this way).

## 6. Summary (`code/summarize_2b.py`; DESIGN §6)
- Subject = unit. Per subject the share of its assignments with p < 0.05 (subjects with no valid p left out of that cell); mean over subjects; subject bootstrap 95% interval (B 20,000, seed 20261024). "Inflated" = lower bound above 10% (rates only).
- **Primary contrast E:** P2 − P5, S-main, balanced, cleaned, main readout, paired at (subject, assignment), averaged per subject, subject bootstrap (no "inflated" flag on a difference).
- **Size-adjusted power (Q2):** for pos_graded under S-main, threshold of each rule = the pooled 5% quantile (lower) of its p over all balanced_graded rows (P3 errors and NaN as p = 1), randomised at the tie so the expected size is 5%; subjects bootstrapped jointly over both arms; reported with the raw power, the threshold, the realised plain size and the tie probability.
- **Fixed-order arms:** one test per subject: rejection rate over subjects with a Wilson interval (with 40 subjects only rates above about 12% separate from 5%); pooled p-value distribution (shares below 0.05, 0.10, 0.20; KS distance from uniform as a descriptive number only, since the p values are discrete).
- **Group level (Q3):** balanced arm, S-main, for each of the 50 assignment indices across subjects: (a) one-sided one-sample t-test of accuracy against 0.5; (b) Stelzer-style group null = mean over subjects of one P5 null accuracy drawn per subject (2,000 draws, seed 20261025). Reported: share of the 50 indices with p < 0.05 for (a) and (b), with a subject-bootstrap interval (200 resamples, 500 group-null draws each).
- Logged: within each subject, the Spearman correlation between an assignment's |correlation of labels with block index| and its P2 rejection, averaged over subjects. The alpha-power slope over time (DESIGN §6 E6) is computed from the features in the results note.
