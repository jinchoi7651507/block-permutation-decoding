# Provenance

The code and outputs in `analyses/` are those of the analyses reported in the paper, and `figures/` draws every figure from them.

Paths written as `<repo>`, `<home>` or `<tmp>` stand for folders on the machines that ran the analyses. The run archives (`*.tgz`) and `step3-ibl/runs_ibl.sha`, `runs_pilot.sha` are byte copies of what those machines wrote and keep their paths and user names.

The sources of the recordings and of the reanalyzed studies' data are listed under Data in `README.md`; the toolbox excerpts are listed in `THIRD-PARTY.md`.
